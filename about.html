Who We Are

The Guwahati Marathon stands as the largest mass participation run in Northeast India and the only AIMS-certified Full Marathon of Assam — a proud symbol of endurance, unity, and community spirit.

With two successful editions to its name, the Guwahati Marathon has evolved into one of India's most spirited running festivals, bringing together professional athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and first-time runners alike.

More than just a race, the Guwahati Marathon is a movement — a festival of fitness, togetherness, and pride that inspires every participant to run not just for distance, but for purpose.

The event is promoted by Guwahati-based company Paper Mache Pvt. Ltd., a sports management company dedicated to building participative sports experiences that inspire people to move, connect, and celebrate.
