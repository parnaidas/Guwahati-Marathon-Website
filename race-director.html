Race Director

The person behind the route, the timing, and the meticulous orchestration of race day.

SS
Surpreet Singh Khalsa
Race Director · "Suri Paaji"

Surpreet Singh Khalsa, known as Suri Paaji, is a prominent figure in the sports industry, driven by his passion for running. Since 2013, he’s made a significant impact as a runner, race director, and event organizer. Over 15 years, Surpreet has completed more than 50 races, including half marathons and 100 km Ultra Marathons, with highlights like the 2017 international 100 km race in Hong Kong.

His expertise extends to directing, route designing, and course measuring for over 90 national and international races, solidifying his reputation as a key player in the running community. His journey from a passionate runner to a key figure in event management underscores his dedication to both the sport and the meticulous orchestration behind successful races.
